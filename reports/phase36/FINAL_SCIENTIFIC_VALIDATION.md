This report delivers the comprehensive scientific validation of the complete HAZARDX AI prediction stack following Phases 29 through 36.

## 2. Scientific Principles Implemented and Verified

1. **Physical Precondition Gating:**
   Models are only evaluated when underlying physical energy sources exist (e.g. earthquake aftershocks require a mainshock; cyclonic winds require a warm-core maritime vortex; flood inundation requires rainfall or river surcharge).
2. **Bayesian Prior Calibration:**
   Models trained on balanced 1:1 case-control historical disaster inventories are properly calibrated using empirical base rates, eliminating systemic over-prediction on peaceful days.
3. **Decoupling Susceptibility from Event Occurrence:**
   High baseline susceptibility (e.g. steep mountain slopes in the Western Ghats or Andes) does not trigger early warning alarms without a coincident dynamic trigger (e.g. monsoon cloudburst or seismic ground motion).
4. **Adherence to International Physical Standards:**
   Wildfire ignition danger is anchored to the Canadian Forest Fire Weather Index (FWI) system and verified against NASA FIRMS satellite thermal anomalies. Earthquake aftershock decay adheres to Omori-Utsu / Reasenberg-Jones formulations.
5. **Strict Governance Over Unreliable Models:**
   Models with negative coefficient of determination (such as earthquake aftershock count and wildfire duration) remain strictly quarantined from operational service.

## 3. Scientific Defensibility Statement

HAZARDX AI does not fabricate real-time data, invent non-existent earthquakes or storms, or assign arbitrary risk percentages. All risk outputs reflect mathematically sound, empirically validated probabilities that civil defense authorities, meteorologists, and the public can rely upon.
