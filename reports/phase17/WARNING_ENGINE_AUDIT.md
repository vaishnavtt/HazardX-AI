## 1. WARNING ENGINE ARCHITECTURE & GOVERNANCE

The HAZARDX AI early-warning system translates multi-source telemetry and machine learning inferences into life-safety alerts. In compliance with Phase 17 directives:
- Warnings **must depend on validated hazard-specific criteria**.
- Arbitrary weighted averages or uncalibrated model scores are strictly prohibited from determining warning severity.
- Full warning lifecycles (`NEW`, `UPDATED`, `ESCALATED`, `RESOLVED`, `EXPIRED`) must be programmatically enforced.

## 2. STANDARDIZED FOUR-TIER SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION

Every alert generated by the system is assigned to one of four standardized severity tiers:

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SEVERITY LEVEL | ACTION PROTOCOL                                  | COLOR CODE          |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| CRITICAL       | Immediate life-safety threat. Immediate          | RED (#DC2626)       |
|                | evacuation or shelter-in-place required.         |                     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| HIGH           | Severe damage potential. Active civil defense    | ORANGE (#EA580C)    |

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 3. HAZARD-SPECIFIC CRITERIA SPECIFICATIONS

In `backend/app/alerts/alert_rules.py`, deterministic rules are paired with calibrated ML thresholds:

### 3.1 Floods Rule Engine
- **CRITICAL (RED):**
  - River discharge ratio $Q_{\text{forecast}} / Q_{\text{bankfull}} \ge 1.50$, OR
  - Calibrated flood probability $\ge 0.85$ with predicted severe inundation (Class 2).
- **HIGH (ORANGE):**
  - River discharge ratio $\ge 1.20$, OR flood probability $\ge 0.65$.
- **MODERATE (YELLOW):**
  - River discharge ratio $\ge 1.00$ (bankfull threshold reached), OR flood probability $\ge 0.40$.
- **LOW (GREEN):**
  - Baseline streamflow ($Q / Q_{\text{mean}} < 1.00$) and flood probability $< 0.40$.

### 3.2 Landslides Rule Engine
- **CRITICAL (RED):**
  - 48h precipitation forecast $\ge 150\text{ mm}$ on slope $> 30^\circ$ with $API_{30\text{d}} \ge 80\text{ mm}$, OR
  - Landslide occurrence probability $\ge 0.75$ with High Magnitude classification.
- **HIGH (ORANGE):**
  - 24h precipitation $\ge 80\text{ mm}$ on steep slope, OR occurrence probability $\ge 0.50$.
- **MODERATE (YELLOW):**
  - 24h precipitation $\ge 40\text{ mm}$, OR occurrence probability $\ge 0.35$.

### 3.3 Tropical Cyclones Rule Engine (Dual-Trigger)
- **CRITICAL (RED):**
  - Maximum sustained wind $V_{\max} \ge 96\text{ kt}$ (Category 3+ Major Hurricane), OR
  - Rapid Intensification probability $\ge 0.45$.
- **HIGH (ORANGE):**
  - Gale-to-hurricane force winds $64\text{ kt} \le V_{\max} < 96\text{ kt}$, OR RI probability $\ge 0.30$.
- **MODERATE (YELLOW):**
  - Tropical storm force winds $34\text{ kt} \le V_{\max} < 64\text{ kt}$, OR RI probability $\ge 0.20$.

### 3.4 Wildfires Rule Engine
- **CRITICAL (RED):**
  - FWI $\ge 50.0$ with relative humidity $< 12\%$ and wind gusts $\ge 50\text{ km/h}$, OR
  - Calibrated wildfire susceptibility $\ge 0.80$ with active high-confidence FIRMS detections within 10 km.
- **HIGH (ORANGE):**
  - FWI $\ge 35.0$ and RH $< 20\%$, OR susceptibility $\ge 0.60$.
- **MODERATE (YELLOW):**
  - FWI $\ge 20.0$ and RH $< 30\%$, OR susceptibility $\ge 0.40$.

### 3.5 Earthquakes Aftershock Rule Engine
- **HIGH (RED):**
  - Mainshock $M_w \ge 7.0$ with depth $\le 30\text{ km}$ and calibrated aftershock probability $\ge 0.68$.
- **MODERATE (YELLOW):**
  - Mainshock $5.5 \le M_w < 7.0$ with aftershock probability $\ge 0.40$.

## 4. COMPLETE ALERT LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT

Managed in `backend/app/alerts/alert_manager.py` and stored in SQLite `alerts` & `alert_audit_logs`:

```
[Ingestion Stream / Forecast Run]
             |
             v
[Deduplication Hash Check] 
   |-- Match Found (Active Alert within 12h) 
   |      |-- Severity Increased? -> Transition to ESCALATED (Audit Logged)
   |      |-- Same Severity?      -> Transition to UPDATED (Refresh Telemetry)
   |
   |-- No Match Found
          v
   Create NEW Alert (Status: ACTIVE)
          |
          v
   [Operator Workflow]
          |-- Acknowledge -> Transition to ACKNOWLEDGED
          |-- Evacuation Cleared -> Transition to RESOLVED
          |
          v
   [Background Sweeper]
          |-- Age > 48h without update -> Transition to EXPIRED
```

### Deduplication Hashing Specification:
$$\text{DedupKey} = \text{MD5}(\text{hazard\_type} + \text{round}(\text{lat}, 2) + \text{round}(\text{lon}, 2) + \text{horizon})$$
This guarantees that successive 5-minute automated polling passes do not flood databases or client notifications with redundant records.
